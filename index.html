About Me

I started on the shop floor — two years as an Assembly Shop Technician at Hyundai Motor India, where every vehicle had to pass the same standard, every time. That discipline — checklists, root-cause fixes, zero tolerance for "close enough" — is the same discipline I now bring to code.

I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology at K. Ramakrishnan College of Engineering (2023–2027), after an earlier Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering. In between, I've been teaching myself web development, databases, and cloud fundamentals — and shipping real projects instead of just tutorials.

Right now I'm building Manganikka, an enterprise-grade creator portfolio & media kit site for a Dubai-based Tamil YouTube creator, on Next.js and TypeScript — my first production-style client build.
